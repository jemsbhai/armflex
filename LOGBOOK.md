# armflex Experimental Logbook

Single source of truth for what was done, when, why, and what happened.

## Rules

- Numbering starts at EXP-101. This avoids collision with wattwarden
  EXP-001 through EXP-005; those results stay in the wattwarden logbook
  and are cited here by ID, never duplicated.
- Append-only. Past entries are never edited or deleted; corrections are
  dated addenda that reference the original entry.
- The plan comes first. Hypothesis, independent and dependent variables,
  controls, protocol, environment, and explicit predictions are written
  and committed before any run.
- Predicted values are labeled predicted. Measured values carry the
  measurement method and an uncertainty estimate.
- Failures are logged with the same care as successes.
- Every logbook update gets its own commit.
